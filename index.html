03 — THE TURNING YEAR

Every season has
its own magic.

There is no wrong time to go. Only different ways to feel it.

❀ March — May

Spring

Wildflowers wake the meadows and every trail hums with birdsong.

☀ June — August

Summer

Long golden evenings, cold lake swims and nights under the stars.

❦ September — November

Autumn

Forests turn to fire and the air carries the scent of woodsmoke.

❄ December — February

Winter

Silent snowfall, crisp mornings and the deepest kind of stillness.

04 — WANDER WELL

Leave it better
than you found it.

The wild gives us so much. A few simple habits help keep it wild for everyone who comes after.

  1. 01

    Plan ahead

    Know the weather, the trail and your limits before you set out.

  2. 02

    Stay on the path

    Fragile ground recovers slowly. Let your footsteps follow the trail.

  3. 03

    Pack it out

    Everything you carry in comes home with you — every last wrapper.

  4. 04

    Respect wildlife

    Watch from a distance, keep quiet and never feed the animals.
